## Using this in an examination

**Do not put the calculator versions on a machine you take into an exam unless
your examination board says you may.**

An fx-CG50 is itself an approved calculator for many examinations — but
examination boards generally require that the calculator's memory be cleared
and that no additional programs or add-ins be present. Both the
[add-in](modules/casio-addin) and the [Python port](modules/casio-fx-cg50)
are exactly the sort of thing those rules exist to exclude, and an invigilator
finding either could treat it as malpractice.

Rules differ between boards, between subjects and between years. Check yours,
and if you are unsure, clear the calculator before the exam. Whatever you
decide is your responsibility, not the author's.

This project is for studying, for homework and for the fun of building it.
